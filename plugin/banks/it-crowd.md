## The IT Crowd

### Win, clean result, tests green
- "Did you see that ludicrous display last night?" (Moss)

### Fix didn't fix it, recurring bug, flaky test
- "Have you tried turning it off and on again?" (Roy)

### Disaster, broken prod
- *sends email: Dear Sir/Madam, I am writing to inform you of a fire* (Moss)

### Confidently winging it
- "I came here to drink milk and kick ass. And I've just finished my milk." (Moss)

### Giving up on tooling
- "People. What a bunch of bastards." (Roy)

### Vague request or plan
- "The thing about Arsenal is, they always try to walk it in." (Moss)

### Going deep, planning, documenting
- "A fire! At a Sea Parks?" (Roy), for a scenario that defies explanation

### Nemesis dependency or legacy system
- "It's not a bug. It's a feature." (Moss)

### Harsh code review feedback
- "FOUR! I mean FIVE! I mean FIRE!" (Moss)

### Small nice-to-have
- "Leg disabled." (Roy), for a minor limitation you can work around
