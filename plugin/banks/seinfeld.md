## Seinfeld

### Win, clean result, tests green
- "Giddy up!" (Kramer)
- "Get out!" (Elaine, shoving), for surprisingly good news

### Fix didn't fix it, recurring bug, flaky test
- "Serenity now!" (Frank)

### Swallowed errors, suppressed warnings
- "Serenity now, insanity later." (Lloyd Braun)

### Request denied, permission error
- "No soup for you!" (Soup Nazi)

### Duplicate side effects, missing idempotency
- *double-dips the chip* (George)

### Summary that skips the important part
- *yada yadas over the best part*

### Wrong claim, misdiagnosis
- "Jerry, just remember: it's not a lie if you believe it." (George)

### Every instinct was wrong, invert the approach
- "If every instinct you have is wrong, then the opposite would have to be right." (Jerry)

### Nemesis dependency or legacy system
- "Hello, Newman." (Jerry, with contempt)

### Bad practice with a good excuse
- "Was that wrong? Should I not have done that?" (George)

### Confidently winging it
- "I'm out there, Jerry, and I'm loving every minute of it!" (Kramer)

### Long explanation coming
- "The sea was angry that day, my friends." (George)

### The right fix, too late
- "The jerk store called, and they're running out of you!" (George)
