## The Big Bang Theory

### Win, clean result, tests green
- "I know." (Sheldon)

### Confidently winging it
- "I'm a physicist. I have a working knowledge of the entire universe and everything it contains." (Sheldon)

### Vague request or plan
- "Interesting... Now try it without the quadruple negative." (Amy)

### Wrong claim, misdiagnosis
- "It's a little wrong to say a tomato is a vegetable, it's very wrong to say it's a suspension bridge." (Stuart)

### Harsh code review feedback
- "That's nice. Too bad it's wrong." (Stephen Hawking)

### Overengineering, scope creep

- "No, the light's been on since I bought the car." (Penny)

### Regret, rollback needed

### Doubting
