## Young Sheldon

### Win, clean result, tests green
- *tastes the first bite of a dish he has been perfecting for fifteen years and is, for one second, completely happy* (George Sr.)

### Concise solution
- "Not a shortcut, a more elegant and efficient method to achieve the correct answer." (Sheldon), for defending a one-liner in review

### Diagnosis proved right
- "I'd like to tell you a story about a time I was right." (Adult Sheldon)

### Disaster, broken prod
- "You waited 'til I had a beer to tell me this story, didn't you?" (George Sr.), for bad news timed at the worst possible moment

### Repeating advice that was ignored
- "Despite my repeated assurances that I could solve the problem with a brain scan and some low-voltage electric shocks, I was never given the opportunity. Such a shame." (Adult Sheldon)

### Regret, rollback needed
- "Well, ideally, I'd like to wipe my sister's brain clean and start over, but my mom wouldn't like that." (Sheldon), when the full rewrite is tempting and forbidden

### Long explanation coming
- "I've put together a presentation detailing the reasons why I should be allowed to go to college, and I'd like you to watch it with an open mind." (Sheldon)

### Security
- "I made a real germ-proof hedge of protection." (Sheldon), for defence in depth that looks paranoid until the breach

### Priorities
- "You have to throw away that jelly." (Sheldon), his first words after nearly choking to death

### Overhyped tool or startup
- "It's got 'the Lord' in it. That's something." (Mary), for the one redeeming feature

### Code review
- "He got you there." (Missy), deadpan, when the reviewer wins the point

### Harsh code review feedback
- "But if a summer in Germany has taught me anything, it's that brutal honesty is its own kind of love." (Sheldon)

### Doubting
- "You don't. That's what makes life interesting." (Meemaw), on how you know who to trust
