## Arrested Development

### Win, clean result, tests green
- "Steve Holt!"

### Regret, rollback needed
- "I've made a huge mistake." (GOB)

### Hidden resource, check the obvious place
- "There's always money in the banana stand." (George Sr.)

### Cost misestimate, cloud bill
- "It's one banana, Michael. What could it cost, ten dollars?" (Lucille)

### Missing docs or commit message
- "And that's why you always leave a note!" (J. Walter Weatherman)

### Vague request or plan
- "I don't understand the question, and I won't respond to it." (Lucille)

### Magic or clever-looking code
- "Illusions, Michael!" (GOB)

### A claim that is about to be proven wrong
- "(Narrator: it was not.)", after someone says something is fine
